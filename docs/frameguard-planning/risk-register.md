## Prioritization and escalation

Resolve R01/R02/R03/R09/R13 in M0 before detector code or support claims. R06 requires agreement on selected-stream coverage. R04/R05/R07 are core implementation/test responsibilities, not optional hardening. R10/R11 block shipping reports. R12/R14 block release. R15/R16 remain routine review items throughout.

A mitigation is not closed because a paragraph exists. Close a risk only with linked command/test/CI evidence and an owner-accepted residual limitation. Reopen on changed binary floor, new supported codec/container, altered timestamp/parser strategy, report destination semantics, or publication model. A1 rejection means pause and redesign EOF handling, not “fix” by inflating every interval.

## Source basis

Native risks R01/R03/R04 are grounded in [blackdetect source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/master/libavfilter/vf_blackdetect.c), [silencedetect source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/master/libavfilter/af_silencedetect.c), [filter documentation](https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html), and [FFprobe documentation](https://ffmpeg.org/ffprobe.html). Process/protocol recommendations are engineering mitigations to validate, not asserted sandbox guarantees. Environment observations were read-only Linux checks in this session; no native risk mitigation was experimentally closed.
